## Platform
Web. Responsive Next.js application with a separate printable PDF route.

## Product Truth
Project Rhapsody is an orbital media studio and a Symphony Space program, not a separate legal entity. The supplied partner brief describes live, improvised, unrepeatable works and curated invitations. This is for prospective brand partners and general-interest audiences, not a competing investment raise.

## Confirmed Requirements
The user requests the exact installed Lunar Gravity Card, the brand fonts, colors and spine. Latest direction: a single web composition, copy left, visual right, with a feeling of being in space. The PDF can remain cream and independently designed. Nothing may be pushed or deployed before review. It has its own local repository; remote name and subdomain are undecided.

## Actions
